General

What does CEP stand for?

CEP stands for Colombia, Ethiopia, and Peru—the three countries whose traditional dishes inspire our menu.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?

Yes, we offer both delivery and takeout. You can order directly from our website or through major delivery apps.

Reservations

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for weekends, but walk-ins are always welcome!

How can I book a table?

You can reserve a table by calling us or using the reservation form on our Contact page.

Menu & Dietary Options

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Absolutely! We have several plant-based dishes from each cuisine, including Shiro Wot and Causa.

Are your dishes spicy?

Some of our Ethiopian dishes are naturally spicy, but most of our menu can be customized to suit your taste.

We’re always looking for passionate food lovers! Please see our Contact section for influencer-specific contact information.
