Welcome to CEP, where culture meets flavor. Our name stands for Colombia, Ethiopia, and Peru three rich culinary traditions brought together under one roof. At CEP, we celebrate the heritage and diversity of each country by offering authentic dishes prepared with care, passion, and respect for tradition.

Our mission is to give our guests more than just a meal we want to offer a journey. Whether you're tasting a comforting ajiaco, a flavorful doro wat, or a vibrant bandeja paisa, every dish tells a story. We believe that food is a powerful way to connect people, and we’re proud to create a space where different cultures can be explored through the senses.

We work closely with chefs and cooks from each country, using traditional techniques and high-quality ingredients to ensure every plate is as genuine as it is delicious. Whether you’re a curious first-timer or a nostalgic traveler missing home, CEP is here to welcome you. Come hungry. Leave inspired.
